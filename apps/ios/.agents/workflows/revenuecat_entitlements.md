# RevenueCat compatibility pointer

Use [`$merian-release`](../../../../skills/merian-release/SKILL.md) for an
explicitly requested RevenueCat or App Store product operation. Follow the
[canonical revenue and identity contract](../../../../docs/features-and-hardware/02-revenue-and-identity.md).

Do not rename stable product, entitlement, offering, application, bundle, or
customer identities, and do not grant, delete, reset, or mutate dashboards
without explicit target-specific authorization.

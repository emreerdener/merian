# Naturebook 1.0.3

Merian is now Naturebook. The name is new; your scans, account, subscriptions,
and Explore content stay exactly where they are.

## What's New

- Field trip Events are now available to everyone, with curated seasonal goals,
  completion badges, published entries, and optional Explore hashtag
  suggestions.
- Every account now includes three Pro scans with no calendar expiry, while the
  separate daily Flash scan remains available.
- See how many Pro scans remain in Results and Settings. Saved Pro results stay
  available after all three are used.
- **Save to camera roll** can now automatically preserve new photos and
  original-quality camera videos in Photos.
- Download a single scan or a selected batch to save retained videos alongside
  photos, with clearer success and failure feedback.
- If Wi-Fi appears connected but cannot reach the internet, or your connection
  drops or silently stalls during analysis, an eligible scan is saved within a
  short safety window and resumes automatically instead of appearing as a
  network-timeout result.
- When recovery continues online, the analysis status now stays focused on the
  identification instead of showing background save mechanics.
- Review captures before identifying, with one shared note you can edit in
  Describe. Free scans support one photo or audio recording plus a note; Pro
  scans support two media items plus a note.
- Turn on **Auto-submit scans** in Workspace settings when you prefer to submit
  each new capture immediately. Review is the default.
- The staged tray uses adaptive Liquid Glass where supported, a blue Identify
  button, and confirmation before discarding a composed scan.
- **Expedition mode** is now included for everyone in Workspace settings.
- Photo imports now check availability before selection and cropping, so known
  scan limits appear before you spend time preparing an image. Allowed imports
  now open crop directly without flashing staged controls or adding a separate
  black handoff layer. Crop actions stay clear of the status area, and the
  confirmation action remains readable.
- Scan, Record, and Describe now use a compact icon-based capture selector with
  a full Liquid Glass background, a brighter selected control, a dark active
  icon, native transitions, accessible mode names, and gentle haptic feedback
  when switching modes.

# Naturebook 1.0.3

Merian is now Naturebook. The name is new; your scans, account, subscriptions,
and Explore content stay exactly where they are.

## What's New

- Field trip Events are now available to everyone, with curated seasonal goals,
  completion badges, published entries, and optional Explore hashtag suggestions.
- Every account now includes three Pro scans with no calendar expiry, while the
  separate daily Flash scan remains available.
- See how many Pro scans remain in Results and Settings. Saved Pro results stay
  available after all three are used.
- **Save to camera roll** can now automatically preserve new photos and
  original-quality camera videos in Photos.
- Download a single scan or a selected batch to save retained videos alongside
  photos, with clearer success and failure feedback.
- If Wi-Fi appears connected but cannot reach the internet, or your connection
  drops or silently stalls during analysis, an eligible scan is saved as
  **Queued for later** within a short safety window and resumes automatically
  instead of appearing as a network-timeout result.
